ClassPulseAI

Version 2 — Rule-Based Draft Behavior-Support Planning Tool

How this prototype works: ClassPulseAI uses predefined browser rules rather than a live AI or LLM service. It organizes educator-entered information into a structured draft for educator review.
Scope: ClassPulseAI helps educators identify and organize the information needed to develop a measurable behavior goal and an appropriate positive replacement behavior. It intentionally does not invent a numerical goal or automatically select a replacement behavior when the entered evidence is not sufficient to support those decisions.
Privacy reminder: Enter only de-identified educational information. Do not enter student names, student identification numbers, diagnoses, medical information, or unnecessary information about other students.
